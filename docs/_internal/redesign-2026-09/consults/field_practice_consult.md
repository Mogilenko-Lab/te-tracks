## Q3. Genic-context metadata

**Evidence and recommendation.** The Q5 relation already covers most inspected requests: overlapping transcript bodies, exon/intron segments, promoter overlap, relative strand, signed TSS/TES distances, exon-cut ties and complete status (`C/gene_child_consult.md:43-51`). Retain its all-isoform evidence and the added gene-body geometry for the bulk 10/20-kb questions (`:55-59`; `K/docs/LIMITATIONS.md:125-137`). TEtranscripts/TElocal use exon support; TEsingle uses transcript spans; scTE folds `UTR` into exon support and removes whole loci; MATES uses gene-body exclusion or intron containment (Q2 source paths). The thesis join is stranded and splice-block-aware, but emits annotation identities rather than a UTR/promoter/context table (`H/eqc_generation/scripts/annotate.sh:26-35`).

Add **UTR overlap as simultaneous flags, bp and exact segments** under each record×transcript relation, with `utr5`, `utr3`, and unsupported/unknown direction states. GENCODE has generic `UTR` rows, not separate 5′/3′ labels. M4 confirms 186,133 rows; strand plus transcript CDS bounds classify 95,682 upstream-in-transcript-orientation and 90,451 downstream. Record any incomplete coding-boundary evidence; exon sequence of a noncoding transcript is not automatically UTR. UTR remains a subset of exonic context rather than an exclusive fourth category competing with exon/intron. M4 finds 10,247 records overlapping derived 5′ UTR and 46,588 overlapping 3′ UTR, unstranded and with overlaps across transcripts retained only as presence per record.

Retain `same|opposite|unknown` relative strand to each candidate host transcript; isoforms/overlapping genes can give several relationships. Keep exon-cut distance with explicit cut coordinates, ties and touching status: it supports read-junction/proximity queries even though these tools generally use overlap directly. Add a separate gene-body table/distance, as already recommended, rather than relabeling TSS distance as “distance to gene.” Host-gene promoter intervals and the TE's own promoter candidates are separate reference relations (`C/gene_child_consult.md:47,59`; `D:229,235`).

Promoter windows and sub-peak distances are explicit programme requirements (`C/gene_context_material.md:52-57`); the inspected annotation readers mainly use overlap/support geometry. MATES defaults to `--cut_mode 5prime --cut_length 1000`, but its clipping code chooses the genomic left end without checking TE strand. That is a named reference truncation, with no validated TE-promoter interpretation; a te-tracks end-window view should use the explicit strand rule in Q5 (`X/MATES/build_reference.py:21-22,142-160`).

**Options and consequences.** A scalar Exonic/Intronic/Intergenic label is useful for reporting, but erases overlapping-isoform relationships unless its derivation and ambiguity flags travel with it. TE-Seq's label is reported second-hand in the owner's toolkit, not verified against TE-Seq source here (`K/docs/METHODOLOGY.md:183`; `C/gene_context_material.md:116-119,329-342`). scTE-style deletion changes the counted universe; annotation flags preserve the full competing feature set for joint inference. Recommend geometry plus a consumer-owned, versioned summary label; defer universal promoter widths, spliced distances, expression-dependent “host gene,” and autonomous/passive labels (`C/gene_context_adr_brief.md:51-81`).

**Settling measurement.** Extend the existing context gate with both strands, UTR/CDS boundaries, incomplete CDS, noncoding transcripts, UTR in one isoform/intron in another, touching exon cuts and tied hosts. Require each transcript's UTR support to lie within its exon union, and compare context flags against each consumer's actual counting GTF after alias normalization. Quantify label discordance and UTR-pair/intersection storage on chr1 before full mm39. The prior estimated 22.5M sparse pairs remains planning evidence under its earlier build, not a fresh acceptance measurement (`C/gene_child_consult.md:49-51,200-216`; M0).

## Q5. The three datasets

**Whole-cell 5′ PE OCM.** The owner's spleen/lung libraries share 20 animals, four per condition, and differ in Cell Ranger version; tissue pairing is by MouseID (`C/thesis_2023_excerpts.md:12-14`). Recommend a harmonized TE reference, gene-model policy and chemistry-qualified alignment/counting recipe, retaining original pipeline provenance. Analyze within tissue at animal×cell-type resolution and use the animal pairing for cross-tissue questions. This statistical recommendation follows the supplied design; cells are subsamples of animals. Barcode/UMI positions, retained cDNA portions of each read, OCM demultiplexing and strand conventions must come from the actual library recipe/BAM evidence, which was not supplied for inspection here.

The dissertation identifies 5′ capture as a route to promoter-source discrimination and also records disappointing older-kit experience (`C/thesis_2023_excerpts.md:316-325`). **Corrections to the supplied notes, established from source and M5.**

- The graph links every cell's UMIs at Hamming distance one, using connected components without annotation-compatibility or abundance constraints.
