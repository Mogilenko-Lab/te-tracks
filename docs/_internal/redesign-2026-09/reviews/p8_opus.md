# Phase 8 re-review (Opus)
Verdict: FAIL

Every acceptance number in plan 18 is confirmed by independent recomputation, and the published child's twelve SAFs are correct row-for-row. The FAIL is verification depth and fixture coverage: two verifier bindings and three fixture cases that plan 18 requires (two of them made explicit in the amendments committed to plan 18 at ~16:2x today, while this review was running) are absent.

## Blocking

- [B1] `src/lib/gene_child.py:385-390` — `verify_products` binds each SAF only by its row count, its declared `distinct_features` and its header line; no code compares a SAF's `Chr`, `Start`, `End`, `Strand` or `GeneID` values with the registration, and the namespace renaming is never recomputed. Plan item 6 requires the SAF rows to equal the registration rows per grouping and universe (now amended to "equal, as a multiset, … joined to the record's strand and contig renamed for the namespace, and each SAF is sorted by the declared order"). **Evidence:** on the tiny publication fixture I moved one SAF row from `chr1 2..2` onto the masked exon `chr1 3..3`, refreshed the `exports.tsv` digest and the two inventory entries, recomputed `BUILD_ID`, and both `verify_build` and `99_verify.py` exited 0 at `BUILD_ID 6f492ec154f5325e600b8348f07e51c261481ba45a95a887b0ada05c3f961721` — an exon-overlapping published row inside a self-consistent archive. The digests pin whatever bytes the writer produced, so a writer defect survives transfer. The real build is unaffected: all twelve SAFs match the registration joined to the parent's contig and strand, with feature ids reproduced from `taxonomy_canonical.tsv` and all eight renamed files exact. **Fix:** in `verify_products`, compare each SAF's full row multiset against the registration joined to parent strand and the namespace alias, and assert the declared sort order; add the rehashed mutation to check 82.
- [B2] `build.sh:65` — the symlinked-parent refusal is bypassed by a trailing slash: `[ ! -L "$PARENT_DIR" ]` follows the link when the path ends in `/`, and `realpath -m` then erases the evidence before `validate_parent`. **Evidence:** `--parent-dir <link>` exits 2 with usage; `--parent-dir <link>/` passes parent verification, creates the child directory and runs all three stages. The `current` clause at `build.sh:64` is lexical and does hold — I refused `…/current`, `…/current/` and `…/./current`, all exit 2 — so the plan's original letter survived; the amended plan now states the lexical rule for symlinks too. **Fix:** strip trailing slashes and judge the lexical path before any symlink resolution; cover both spellings in check 82.
- [B3] `tests/fixtures/gene/geometry.json` — the twelve cases cover every geometry bullet of plan item 7 as the implementer received it, and miss the three the amendment adds: a nested exon, two overlapping exons of different extents (`Adjacent` is abutting at 1550/1551; `Duplicate` is identical), and a record cut into four blocks by two separate mask intervals. **Evidence:** I enumerated these three gaps from the fixture before reading the codex review; `subtract_batch`'s internal-gap arm (`counts[index] = len(blocks)` with two or more intervals inside one record) and `annotation_mask`'s `max(end, merged[-1][1])` widening arm are exercised by no hand-authored answer. **Fix:** add the three cases with their expected blocks, statuses and coverage rows.
